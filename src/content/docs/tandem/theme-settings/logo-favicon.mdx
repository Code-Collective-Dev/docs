Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize**, then click the paint brush icon for **Theme settings** and choose **Logo and favicon**.

The logo set here is used on the password page and gift cards, and in the header unless the [Header](/tandem/global/header/) has its own. Upload an SVG or a PNG at least twice the size it shows at. The favicon should be a square image, ideally 32 x 32 px PNG.

## Settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo | Image | Used in the header, on the password page and on gift cards. The header can use a different logo in its own settings. |
| Favicon | Image | 32 x 32px .png recommended. |
