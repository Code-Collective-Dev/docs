Tandem shows stars from the standard Shopify rating metafields, so it works with most review apps, including Judge.me, Okendo, Yotpo and Shopify's Product Reviews.

<Steps>

1. Install a review app and turn on its option to sync ratings to Shopify metafields (`reviews.rating` and `reviews.rating_count`).
2. Turn on **Show star rating** in **Theme settings > Motion and cards** for product cards.
3. Add the **Rating** block to the product page. Set **Reviews section ID** to the ID of the app's review widget so clicking the stars scrolls to it.
4. Add the app's review widget to the product page as an **App** block or in the **Apps** section.

</Steps>

## Without an app

Create two product metafields, **Rating** (`custom.rating`, type Rating) and **Rating count** (`custom.rating_count`, type Integer), and fill them in by hand. Products without a rating show no stars.
