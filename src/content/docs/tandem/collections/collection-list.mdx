The collection list page shows all your collections as image cards at `/collections`. Collections without an image use their first product's image.

## Section settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heading | Text | Leave empty to hide it. |
| Products per row on desktop | Slider | 2 to 5. |
| Collections per page | Slider | 8 to 48. |
| Color scheme | Color scheme | One of the schemes from Theme settings > Colors. |
