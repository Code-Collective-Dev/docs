The **Variant picker** block shows each product option, such as size or color. Choose buttons or dropdowns in its **Style** setting.

## Color swatches

Tandem shows swatches automatically for options that use Shopify's swatch values.

<Steps>

1. Open a product in **Products** and edit the option, such as Color.
2. Pick values from the category metafield list, such as Color, or create your own with a color or image.
3. Save. The variant picker shows each value as a round swatch, and sold-out values are crossed out.

</Steps>

See Shopify's help on [variant swatches](https://help.shopify.com/manual/products/variants/swatches).
