Founder video plays a video of a founder or maker next to a quote, their name, role, portrait and signature. Upload a video or paste a YouTube or Vimeo link. The video only loads when the shopper presses play.

## Add the section

<Steps>

1. Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize** and go to any page.
2. Click **Add section** and choose **Founder video**.
3. Fill in the content, then click **Save**.

</Steps>
