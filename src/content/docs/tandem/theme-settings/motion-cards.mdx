Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize**, then click the paint brush icon for **Theme settings** and choose **Motion and cards**.

Product cards appear in collections, search results, featured collections and recommendations, so these settings apply everywhere at once.

Tag a product `badge:Bestseller` or `badge:New` to show a label on its card when **Show badges from tags** is on. Sold out and Sale labels take priority.

## Settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reveal sections on scroll | On or off | Sections fade and rise in as they come into view. Turned off for shoppers who prefer reduced motion. |
| **Product cards** | | |
| Hover effect | Choice | Zoom image / Lift card / None. |
| Blend white image backgrounds | On or off | Product shots on white take on the tile color from the color scheme. Best for cut-out product photos. |
| Quick add | On or off | A button on each card. Single-variant products go straight to the cart; others open a short size picker. |
| Show star rating | On or off | Reads the product rating your review app fills in (reviews.rating). Without an app, create a Rating metafield (custom.rating) and an Integer count (custom.rating_count) on products and fill them in. |
| Show subtitle | On or off | Shows the product's Subtitle metafield (descriptors.subtitle, or custom.subtitle), such as a hero ingredient or the skin concern it's for. |
| Show badges from tags | On or off | Add a tag like badge:Bestseller or badge:New to show it as a label on the card. Sold out and Sale labels take priority. |
| Badge style | Choice | Subtle / Bold. |
