Quick order lets wholesale customers add many products at once from one table, grouped by product type if you like. Their prices, quantity rules and case packs apply, and **Paste a list** lets them paste SKUs and quantities. Shoppers who aren't signed in see a message with a link to your application page.

Assign the **page.quick-order** template to a page, then edit it in the theme editor.
