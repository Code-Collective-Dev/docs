Tandem treats a shopper as a wholesale customer when they're signed in as a contact of a company in Shopify B2B. From then on, the theme shows them their own catalog prices, quantity rules and volume pricing everywhere: product cards, product pages, the cart, quick order and buy again.

B2B features depend on your Shopify plan. See Shopify's help on [B2B](https://help.shopify.com/manual/b2b).

## What wholesale customers get

| Feature | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Their own prices and volume pricing | Every page |
| Quantity rules, such as cases of 6 | Product pages, cart, quick order |
| A Retail / Wholesale switch | [Header](/tandem/wholesale/header-switch/) |
| A home base with shortcuts and recent orders | [Wholesale portal](/tandem/wholesale/wholesale-portal/) |
| Bulk ordering from one table | [Quick order](/tandem/wholesale/quick-order/) |
| Reorder anything they've bought before | [Buy again](/tandem/wholesale/buy-again/) |
| Downloads such as line sheets | [Wholesale resources](/tandem/wholesale/wholesale-resources/) |
| Content only they can see | [Show to](/tandem/wholesale/audience/) |

## What other shoppers get

Retail shoppers see your normal prices, plus an invitation to apply: the [wholesale application form](/tandem/wholesale/wholesale-application/), the **Wholesale pricing** block on product pages and the wholesale link in the header.

## Set it up

1. [Set up B2B in your admin](/tandem/wholesale/setup-b2b/).
2. [Turn on the header switch](/tandem/wholesale/header-switch/).
3. Build your trade page with the application form, terms and portal.
4. Add the quick order and buy again pages.
