Buy again lists everything a signed-in customer has ordered, newest first, with the last quantity ready to add. Wholesale customers see their own prices and quantity rules. Below the list are their past orders, each with a reorder button.

Assign the **page.reorder** template to a page. It works for retail customers too.

## Section settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heading | Text | Leave empty to hide it. |
| Text | Rich text | Supporting copy under the heading. |
| Orders to look through | Slider | 5 to 50. The most recent orders are used. |
| Show past orders | On or off |  |
| Color scheme | Color scheme | One of the schemes from Theme settings > Colors. |
| Card color scheme | Color scheme | The scheme used inside the cards, so they can stand out from the section background. |
