You can try Tandem for free before you buy. A trial theme can be customized but not published until you purchase it.

## Add the theme

<Steps>

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Click **Visit Theme Store** and search for **Tandem**, or open the Tandem listing directly.
3. Pick a preset (Tandem, Larder or Halden) and click **Try theme**, or **Buy theme** if you're ready.
4. Tandem is added to your theme library as an unpublished theme.

</Steps>

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. Click **Customize** next to Tandem to open the theme editor.
2. In **Theme settings** (the paint brush icon), add your logo, then set your [colors](/tandem/theme-settings/colors/) and [fonts](/tandem/theme-settings/typography/).
3. Build your main menu and footer menu in **Content > Menus**, then select them in the [Header](/tandem/global/header/) and [Footer](/tandem/global/footer/).
4. Edit the home page sections. Use the template picker at the top of the editor to set up the product, collection and page templates.
5. Assign the ready-made page templates to your pages, such as **page.contact** and **page.trade**. See [Pages](/tandem/pages/pages/).
6. Check your store on a phone with the device toggle at the top of the editor.

</Steps>

## Publish

When you're happy, go back to **Online Store > Themes**, click the **...** menu next to Tandem and choose **Publish**.

<Aside type="tip">
Before you publish, place a test order to check checkout, emails and shipping. See Shopify's guide to [placing a test order](https://help.shopify.com/manual/checkout-settings/test-orders).
</Aside>
