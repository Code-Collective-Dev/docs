Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize**, then click the paint brush icon for **Theme settings** and choose **Search**.

Predictive search opens a panel from the header and shows products, suggestions, collections, pages and articles as shoppers type.

## Settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show results as shoppers type | On or off | Opens a search panel from the header with products, suggestions, collections, pages and articles. |
| Show product vendor | On or off |  |
| Show product price | On or off |  |
