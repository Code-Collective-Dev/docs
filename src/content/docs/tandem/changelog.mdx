## 1.0.0

First release on the Shopify Theme Store, with three presets: Tandem, Larder and Halden.

- Retail and wholesale in one theme: a Retail / Wholesale switch in the header, wholesale prices and case packs for B2B customers, a wholesale portal, quick order, buy again, a trade application form and wholesale-only content.
- Product page: highlights (typed or from a metafield), delivery estimate, key facts, taste and nutrition scales, size chart, countdown, purchase options for subscriptions, pickup availability, complementary products and an accent color from a metafield.
- Featured product section with rich media, variant picker, buy buttons and app blocks.
- Storytelling sections: scroll story (product shots, images, illustration or a scroll-driven Lottie animation), shoppable image, before and after, timeline, comparison table, stats and video carousel.
- Store locator with filters, contact page with contact details, and a stockist template.
- Collection filtering with Search and Discovery filters, promo tiles in the product grid and load more.
- Cart drawer with free shipping progress, recommendations and order notes.
