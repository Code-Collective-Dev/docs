Recently viewed shows the last products the shopper looked at, newest first. The list is kept in their browser, the product being viewed is left out, and the section stays hidden until there's something to show.

## Add the section

<Steps>

1. Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize** and go to the product template or any page.
2. Click **Add section** and choose **Recently viewed**.
3. Fill in the content, then click **Save**.

</Steps>

## Section settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heading | Text | Leave empty to hide it. |
| Products to show | Slider | 2 to 6. |
| Color scheme | Color scheme | One of the schemes from Theme settings > Colors. |
