Related articles goes at the end of an article. Posts that share a tag with the current one come first, then the newest posts fill any gaps. Outside an article it shows the newest posts from the chosen blog.

## Add the section

<Steps>

1. Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize** and go to the article template.
2. Click **Add section** and choose **Related articles**.
3. Fill in the content, then click **Save**.

</Steps>

## Section settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heading | Text | Leave empty to hide it. |
| Blog | Blog | Used on pages that aren't articles. On an article page, posts come from the article's own blog. |
| Posts to show | Slider | 2 to 4. |
| Show date | On or off |  |
| Show reading time | On or off |  |
| Show "View all" link | On or off | Links to the full collection or blog. |
| Color scheme | Color scheme | One of the schemes from Theme settings > Colors. |
