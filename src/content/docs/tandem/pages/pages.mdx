Pages show the title and content you write in **Online Store > Pages**. Tandem also includes ready-made templates you can assign in each page's **Theme template** setting:

| Template | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| page.about | Your story, with sections like founder video, timeline and stats |
| page.contact | A contact form with your details |
| page.stockists | The store locator |
| page.trade | Your wholesale program and application form |
| page.quick-order | Bulk ordering for wholesale customers |
| page.reorder | Buy again for signed-in customers |

## Section settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show title | On or off |  |
| Content width | Choice | Narrow / Wide. |
| Show to | Choice | Everyone / Everyone except wholesale customers / Wholesale customers only. Wholesale customers are shoppers signed in to a B2B company. The section always shows in the theme editor. |
| Color scheme | Color scheme | One of the schemes from Theme settings > Colors. |
