You change almost everything in Tandem from the theme editor, without touching code. Open it from **Online Store > Themes > Customize**.

## Sections and blocks

A **section** is a full-width part of a page, such as a hero, a product grid or an FAQ. A **block** is a piece inside a section, such as one slide, one question or one card.

- Click **Add section** to add a section to the page.
- Drag the handle next to a section or block to reorder it.
- Click the eye icon to hide a section without deleting it.
- Right-click a section, or use its **...** menu, to duplicate or remove it.

## Templates

Each type of page uses a template: the home page, product pages, collection pages, pages, blog posts and so on. Use the picker at the top of the editor to switch templates. You can create extra templates, such as a second product template, and assign them to individual products or pages.

## Header and footer groups

The header and footer are section groups. Sections you add there, such as an announcement bar or a sale countdown bar, show on every page.

## Color schemes

Every section has a **Color scheme** setting. You define the schemes once in [Theme settings > Colors](/tandem/theme-settings/colors/), then pick one per section. Changing a scheme updates every section that uses it.

## Show to

Most sections have a **Show to** setting: Everyone, Everyone except wholesale customers, or Wholesale customers only. See [Show content to one audience](/tandem/wholesale/audience/).

<Aside>
Sections set to one audience always show in the theme editor, so you can edit them.
</Aside>
