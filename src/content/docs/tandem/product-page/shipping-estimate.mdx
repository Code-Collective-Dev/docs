The **Shipping info** block can show a line such as "Order within 3 hours to get it Mon, Nov 9 to Wed, Nov 11", worked out from rules you set. It's an estimate shown to shoppers; your real rates and delivery times are set in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**.

## How the date is worked out

1. Orders placed after the **Order cut-off** time count from the next working day.
2. **Days to prepare an order** are added.
3. The fastest and slowest delivery days are added to give a range.
4. Weekends are skipped when **No shipping or delivery at weekends** is on, and so are any **Closed dates** you list, such as holidays.

Times use your store's time zone, set in **Settings > General**.
