The [Scroll story](/tandem/sections/scroll-story/) section can play a Lottie animation that moves with the scroll: scroll down and it plays forward, scroll up and it rewinds.

<Steps>

1. Export your animation as a Lottie `.json` file, for example from After Effects with the Bodymovin plugin, or download one from LottieFiles.
2. In your admin, go to **Content > Files** and upload the file. Copy its link.
3. In the theme editor, select the scroll story and set **Visual** to **Lottie**.
4. Paste the link into **Lottie file link** and choose the **Playback**.
5. Add a **Still image** and an **Animation description** for shoppers who prefer reduced motion and for screen readers.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Keep the file small: under 1 MB is a good target. Animations with embedded photos are larger than pure vector animations.
</Aside>

The Lottie player only loads when the section comes into view, so it doesn't slow down the rest of the page.
