Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize**, then click the paint brush icon for **Theme settings** and choose **Cart**.

Choose whether adding to cart opens a drawer or goes to the cart page. The drawer can show express checkout buttons, payment icons, a free shipping progress bar and product suggestions.

The free shipping bar only shows progress toward the amount you enter here. Set the actual rate in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**, and keep the two in sync.

## Settings

| Setting | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cart type | Choice | Drawer / Page. |
| Show order note | On or off |  |
| Free shipping threshold | Number | Shows a progress bar in the cart. Enter the amount in your store's currency; the bar is hidden for shoppers browsing in other currencies. Set the actual rate in Settings > Shipping and delivery. |
| **Cart drawer** | | |
| Show express checkout buttons | On or off | Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal, as set up in your payment settings. |
| Show payment icons | On or off |  |
| Show product suggestions | On or off | Based on the first item in the cart. Items already in the cart are left out. |
| Suggestions heading | Text |  |
| Suggest | Choice | Related products / Complementary products. Complementary products are the ones you pick in the Search & Discovery app. Without them, Shopify suggests related products. |
| Products in an empty cart | Collection | Shows up to 4 products from this collection when the cart is empty. |
| Empty cart heading | Text |  |
