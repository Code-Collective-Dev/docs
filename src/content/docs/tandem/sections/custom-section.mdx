Custom section is an empty section you fill with theme blocks: groups, text and Custom Liquid. Nest blocks inside groups and set each group's direction, spacing and alignment to build layouts the other sections don't cover.

## Add the section

<Steps>

1. Open **Online Store > Themes > Customize** and go to any page.
2. Click **Add section** and choose **Custom section**.
3. Fill in the content, then click **Save**.

</Steps>

### Theme blocks
