Create a second product template when some products need different blocks, such as linens with a size chart or coffee with a taste scale.

<Steps>

1. In the theme editor, open the template picker and choose **Products > Create template**.
2. Name it, such as **linen**, and base it on the default product template.
3. Change its blocks and sections, then save.
4. In **Products**, open each product that should use it and choose the template under **Theme template**.

</Steps>
