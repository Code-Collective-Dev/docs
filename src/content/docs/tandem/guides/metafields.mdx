Metafields are extra fields on products, such as a care note or a roast level. Tandem reads them so each product page can show its own details without a separate template.

## Create a metafield

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings > Custom data > Products** and click **Add definition**.
2. Give it a name. Shopify suggests a key such as `custom.care`; note it down.
3. Choose the type from the table below, then save.
4. Fill in the value on each product, at the bottom of the product page in your admin.
5. In the theme editor, enter the key in the setting listed below.

</Steps>

## Metafields Tandem reads

| Feature | Setting | Example key | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Accent color](/tandem/guides/accent-color/) | Product information > Accent color metafield | `custom.accent` | Color |
| [Highlights](/tandem/product-page/highlights/) | Highlights block > Metafield | `custom.care` | Single line text, list |
| [Star rating](/tandem/guides/reviews/) | Filled in by your review app | `reviews.rating` | Rating |
| Card subtitle | Theme settings > Motion and cards > Show subtitle | `custom.subtitle` | Single line text |
| Key facts | Key facts block > Fact metafield | `custom.skin_concern` | Any; lists show as "Redness, Dryness" |
| Taste scale score | Taste scale block > Score metafield | `custom.roast` | Integer, 1 to 5 |
| Nutrition rows | Nutrition block > Rows metafield | `custom.nutrition` | Multi-line text |
| Products in an article | Article > Products metafield | `custom.products` | Product list (on blog posts) |

## Connect a dynamic source

Text settings such as a collapsible row's content, a size chart's notes or a countdown's end date can also be connected to a metafield directly. Click the **Connect dynamic source** icon next to the setting and choose the metafield.
