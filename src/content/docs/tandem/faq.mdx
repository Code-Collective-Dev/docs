title: FAQ

### My wholesale customers see retail prices

They need to be signed in as a contact of a B2B company, and the company location needs a catalog that includes the product. A customer tag alone isn't enough. See [Set up B2B](/tandem/wholesale/setup-b2b/).

### A section shows in the editor but not on my store

Check its **Show to** setting. Sections for one audience are hidden from everyone else on the live store but always show in the editor.

### Star ratings don't show

Your review app needs to sync ratings to the `reviews.rating` metafield. See [Ratings and reviews](/tandem/guides/reviews/).

### The scroll story doesn't animate

The Lottie visual needs a link to a Lottie JSON file from **Content > Files**. Shoppers who prefer reduced motion see the beats stacked instead, which is expected.

### Expand to full width while pinned does nothing

The stage grows from the page width to the screen edges, so the growth only shows when the stage has a different color scheme from the section.

### Filters are missing on a collection

Add them in the Search and Discovery app and turn on **Enable filtering** in the Product grid section. Filters only show values that products in that collection have.

### The free shipping bar shows the wrong amount

The bar uses the threshold in **Theme settings > Cart**, not your shipping rates. Keep the two in sync. The bar is hidden for shoppers browsing in another currency.

### Can I use apps on the product page?

Yes. Add an **App** block in Product information or Featured product, or use the Apps section anywhere.

### Does Tandem support more than one language?

Yes. Translate your store with Shopify's Translate and Adapt app. The theme's own text, such as button labels, can be edited in **Online Store > Themes > ... > Edit default theme content**.

### Can I edit the theme code?

You can, from **... > Edit code**, but changes to the code aren't covered by support and aren't carried over when you update. Duplicate the theme first.
